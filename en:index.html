Osake Diary is a personal tasting diary for Japanese spirits — shochu, sake, whiskey, plum wine, and awamori. If something isn't working right, or you're not sure how to do something, this page should help.

Common questions

My entries disappeared after an update.
Your data is almost always still there. Try force-quitting the app and reopening it. If it's still missing, check that you're signed into the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled for the app under Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

How do I get my data onto a new phone?
If both devices are signed into the same Apple ID with iCloud on, everything syncs automatically — no action needed. As a backup, you can also export a backup file from the ⋯ menu and restore it on the new device.

The free trial ended and I want to buy the app.
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Purchase Full Access." It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription — pay once, keep it forever, on every device signed into your Apple ID.

I already paid, but it's asking me to purchase again.
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen. This is common after reinstalling the app or setting it up on a new device.

Can I use the app in Japanese?
Yes. Go to Settings → Language and choose 日本語. You can switch back to English at any time.

Something else

If none of this covers what you're running into, just email me directly with a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot if you can include one.
